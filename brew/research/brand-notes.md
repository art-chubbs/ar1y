## Voice
Dry, precise, anti-hype, British spelling. Short declaratives. Rarely says "AI" up front.
Key lines: "We make the whole video." · "You approve it; you do not assemble it." · "Every frame watched by a person." ·
"A team, not a tool you operate." · "There is nothing here for you to operate." · "A file, not a first draft." ·
"Upload it nine times, not once." · "What tools make. What we make." · "Arrive with nothing."

## Facts to keep accurate
- Pipeline pills: TOPIC → RESEARCH → SCRIPT → VOICE → EDIT → REVIEWED → DELIVERED
- Three days start to finish; two rounds of notes; up to sixty minutes; 15-point review list
- Languages (9): English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Polish, Indonesian, Danish (Japanese, Arabic soon)
- B-roll: self serve, credits, MP4 in ~2–5 min, "your product, in a hand, on the right surface", two free re-rolls
- UGC: vertical, "look shot, not generated", hook / demo / payoff, synthetic cast only, AI label on screen
- Thumbnails: drawn by a person from a frame actually in the video ("no six-fingered hands")
- CTA: "Send us a script" · "Your first video is free" (up to ten minutes, if you bring the voiceover)
- Differentiator example: narration says "Rose Freedman" → a tool gives a stock skyline; brew gives her photograph, 1911.
